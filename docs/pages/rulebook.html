Rulebook

Standard Scrabble flow, tuned for Scrabble.c, including blank tiles, premium squares, and the match hotkeys.

Setup

Each player draws seven tiles. The first word must cover the center star. After that, every new play must connect to tiles already on the board.

Taking a turn

  1. Place tiles from your rack onto the board in a single straight line (row or column).
  2. If you place a blank (wildcard), assign a letter before you submit.
  3. Submit the play. Valid words score; invalid placements are rejected with a warning.
  4. Draw tiles until your rack has seven again (or the bag is empty).

You may also pass or shuffle your rack. Passing skips scoring for that turn. Shuffling returns tiles to the bag and redraws, costing the turn.

Board bonuses

Premium squares apply the first time a tile is placed on them.

  • TW : triple word
  • DW : double word
  • TL : triple letter
  • DL : double letter
  • ★ : center (double word)

Scoring

  • Letter values are printed on each tile. Wildcards score 0.
  • Letter bonuses modify the tile; word bonuses multiply the whole word (after letter bonuses).
  • Bingo: using all seven rack tiles in one play adds a 50-point bonus.
  • When several new words form at once, each is scored.

Wildcards

Blank tiles are wild. Place one, open the letter picker, and choose A-Z. The blank stays worth zero points for the rest of the match. Cancel returns the blank to your rack.

End of match

Play continues until one player clears their rack and the bag is empty, or players agree to stop. Remaining tiles on other racks are deducted from those players’ scores. Highest score wins.

Match controls

  • Submit : confirm the current placement
  • Pass : skip the turn
  • Shuffle : redraw the rack (costs the turn)
  • Pause / Exit : leave the match; use save from the menu when you need a file

Menu shortcuts include New Game, Load, Mute, Settings, Fullscreen, and Quit. Check the hotkey bar at the bottom of each screen.
