What you get

Classic board. Native feel.

Built with raylib and RayGUI, no Electron Runtime were hurt in the process.

  • 01

    Local turn-by-turn

    Pass the mouse. Save when you wanna take a break, load from where you left off.

  • 02

    Wildcards & luxury tiles

    Blank tiles - lets you pick a letter at your wish. Premium squares, TW, DW, TL, DL.

  • 03

    Sound, settings, saves

    SFX and BGM mixers, fullscreen, and local save files so a long match does not vanish on quit.

  • 04

    Network on the way

    LAN multiplayer is in progress. The menu already has the seat reserved for friends on the same network.

In play

A board that stays out of the way.

Rack, score history, and hotkeys sit beside a full 15×15 grid : built for focus, not Chromium.
